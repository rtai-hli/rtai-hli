# Hi, I’m Henan Li

I’m a disability researcher and software developer working on accessible reporting, practical AI tools, and community technology.

At **Human Services Research Institute (HSRI)**, I’m a Research Associate II working on National Core Indicators reporting. I’m also **Co-Founder and Vice President of Research Triangle AI (RTAI)**, where I help bring people together to learn, exchange ideas, and explore useful applications of AI.

## Accessibility and reporting

My work connects research, software, and the publication process. I lead NCI-IDD reporting and delivered 62 reports across 39 states.

I build reporting workflows with R and Python, incorporate chart descriptions into report generation, and develop PDF accessibility checks and repairs using Python and pikepdf. I also work on website accessibility, using WCAG AA as a development guideline and tools such as WAVE and Userback.

## Technology for everyday needs

Some of my projects live in private repositories. They include:

- **School PTA website:** I designed and maintain my daughters’ school PTA website pro bono, helping families find news, events, and resources. I also built a newsletter publishing workflow within the website platform’s constraints.
- **MealViewer:** A tool that brings school lunch menus to a household display through spoken announcements and a visual interface.
- **AI workflow experiments:** Tools for exploring models, organizing knowledge, and making recurring tasks easier.

## Learning and community

Through RTAI, I organize events and host discussions around innovation, education, and connection. I’m interested in helping everyday people, small businesses, and people with disabilities participate in the opportunities AI creates.

[Model Audit Playground](https://github.com/rtai-hli/model-audit-playground) is a hands-on workshop for exploring model behavior: make a prediction, run an experiment, and examine what changes.
